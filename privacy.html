Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

social-uploader ("the app") is a personal tool run by a single creator to publish their own video clips to their own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data the app accesses

  • Login tokens issued by Google (YouTube), Meta (Instagram) and TikTok after the account owner signs in and grants permission. These let the app upload videos to that owner's account.
  • Basic account identifiers that a platform returns as part of signing in (for example an account or channel ID), used only to know which account to post to.
  • The owner's own video files, titles and captions, read from a folder on the owner's computer.

The app does not access viewers' data, comments, messages, followers, contacts, or any other person's account.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and publish the owner's videos to the owner's own accounts, and to keep a local record of which videos were posted. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any other purpose.

Storage

Login tokens and the posting log are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server operated by the app; the app has no server. Videos are sent only to the platform they are being published on (and, for Instagram, to temporary storage needed to give Instagram a link to the video, deleted after publishing).

Sharing

No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties, other than sending each video to the platform it is being posted on.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; by using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time:

  • Google / YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Instagram / Meta: Settings → Apps and websites, or Meta Accounts Center
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions

To have all data held by the app deleted, email the address below. Because all data lives on the owner's own computer, deletion means removing the stored tokens and posting log, which happens within 7 days of a request.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
