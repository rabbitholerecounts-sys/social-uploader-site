social-uploader

A personal tool that posts my own short video clips to my own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts on a schedule.

What it does

  • Takes finished video clips that I created from a folder on my own computer.
  • Uploads each clip, with its title and caption, to my YouTube channel, Instagram account and TikTok account through each platform's official API.
  • Keeps a local log of what was posted so the same clip is never posted twice.

Who it is for

This app is operated by a single creator for their own accounts. It is not offered to the public, does not have other users, and does not access anyone else's accounts or data.

Permissions it uses

It asks each platform only for permission to upload and publish videos to the account owner's own account. It does not read messages, followers, comments, or personal profile details beyond what the platform requires to identify the account.
